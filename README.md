### 👨‍🚀 Senior Software Engineer | Embedded, RTOS & AI Systems

I design and build **high-reliability, performance-critical software** for  
**embedded and space-grade systems**.

My background spans **C/C++, RTOS (RTEMS), Linux**, and **mission-critical system architectures**,  
with hands-on experience in **space payload flight software**, **large-scale engineering platforms**,  
and **AI/ML systems for scientific and industrial applications**.

I enjoy working close to the hardware, understanding systems deeply,  
and turning complex ideas into **robust, verifiable software**.
